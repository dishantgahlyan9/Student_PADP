How the AI Model Processes Features

The prediction engine analyzes 16 baseline features spanning demographics, academic performance, and external pressures. It automatically derives high-level behavioral indicators (such as Attendance Disruption, Academic Risk Index, and GPA Trajectory) before computing dropout probability.

1. Demographics & Background
Age Numeric

Student's age in years.

Valid Range: 17.0 – 35.0
Example: 21.0
Gender Categorical

Gender identity of the student.

Valid Values: Male, Female
Example: Male
Family_Income Numeric

Total annual / household income.

Valid Range: 25,000 – 350,000+
Example: 38000
Internet_Access Categorical

Access to high-speed home internet.

Valid Values: Yes, No
Example: Yes
2. Academic Performance
GPA / Semester_GPA / CGPA Numeric (0-4.0)

Cumulative GPA, current term GPA, and overall grade point averages.

Valid Range: 0.00 – 4.00
Example: 2.85
Semester Categorical

Current enrolled year / academic stage.

Valid Values: Year 1, Year 2, Year 3, Year 4
Example: Year 2
Department Categorical

Enrolled faculty or discipline.

Valid Values: CS, Engineering, Arts, Business, Science
Example: CS
3. Engagement & External Pressures
Attendance_Rate Numeric (%)

Percentage of class attendance.

Valid Range: 0.0 – 100.0%
Example: 85.5
Study_Hours_per_Day Numeric

Self-study hours per day.

Valid Range: 0.5 – 12.0 hrs
Example: 4.0
Assignment_Delay_Days Numeric

Average days late in assignment turn-ins.

Valid Range: 0 – 30 days
Example: 1
Stress_Index Scale (1-10)

Self-reported emotional/academic stress.

Valid Range: 1.0 – 10.0
Example: 5.5
Travel_Time_Minutes Numeric

One-way transit time to university.

Valid Range: 5 – 120 mins
Example: 30
Part_Time_Job Categorical

Whether student works a job while studying.

Valid Values: Yes, No
Example: No
Scholarship Categorical

Whether student is on a scholarship.

Valid Values: Yes, No
Example: No
